SPY VS SPY - a port of Atari XL/XE game for Picocomputer 6502
    
An action and strategy game for one player against the computer or two
players locally. Search rooms, collect the secret items, use traps against
the other spy and escape before your opponent.

STARTING In the menu, Up/Down selects a setting; Left/Right changes its value.
Choose NUMBER OF PLAYERS. In solo play, check COMPUTER IS PLAYER and use
the controls for the human spy. Space, Enter or F2 starts the game.

KEYBOARD
White spy:       Arrow keys to move; Space for fire/action or 1st gamepad.
Black spy:       W/A/S/D to move; Left Shift for fire/action or 2nd gamepad.
F2/Space/Enter:  Atari START.
F3:              Atari SELECT input.
F4:              Return to the menu.
Esc:             Pause during play.
M:               Music on/off; sound effects remain active.
Fire/action is context-dependent: use it with the direction controls to
interact with the game. There are no separate keys for individual actions.

TRAPULATOR Quickly press and release Fire twice to activate the Trapulator, the panel
to the right of your playfield. Use directions to choose a trap or the map,
then press Fire once to confirm.
The Trapulator is unavailable during combat when both spies share a room!

Have fun!
